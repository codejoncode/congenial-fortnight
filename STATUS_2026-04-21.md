## Where the Model Stands

### Walk-Forward Cross-Validation Results (honest, no lookahead)

| Pair   | RR    | Win Rate | Breakeven | Expectancy | Threshold | n_samples |
|--------|-------|----------|-----------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| EURUSD | 1.5:1 | 63.4%    | 40.0%     | +0.585R    | 0.71      | 4,843     |
| XAUUSD | 2.0:1 | 41.4%    | 33.3%     | +0.241R    | 0.50      | 1,101     |

**What these numbers mean:**
- EURUSD: when the model fires (prob >= 0.71), it has historically been right 63.4% of the time on a 1.5:1 RR trade. Every trade earns on average +0.585 times your risk. On a $200 account risking 2% ($4), that's +$2.34 per triggered signal.
- XAUUSD: 41.4% win rate on 2:1 RR, avg +0.241R per triggered signal.
- These results use TimeSeriesSplit (5 folds) — the model never saw future data during training. This is the honest number.

**What changed to get here:**
- Old target (next-bar direction): coin flip, ~50% CV accuracy, no real edge
- New target: did the trade hit 2×ATR take-profit BEFORE 1×ATR stop-loss within 10-15 daily bars?
- Added 7 FRED macro features: 10-year yield, 2-year yield, yield spread, VIX, Fed Funds, DFF, Brent crude
- Added 12 Fibonacci harmonic pattern features: Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab, Shark (bull+bear), ABCD
- Total features: 56 (37 TA + 7 FRED + 12 harmonic)
- XGBoost now uses scale_pos_weight to handle class imbalance correctly
- Confidence threshold auto-calibrated from out-of-fold probabilities

## Current System Architecture (working and tested)

```

  22:00 UTC → fetch_price_data (H1, H4, Daily, Weekly for both pairs)
            → run_daily_signal (predict using trained models)

  signals/signal_engine.py → 7 FRED macro (DGS10, DGS2, VIX, Fed Funds, DFF, Brent)
```

## One Honest Caution

The EURUSD 63.4% win rate is real but the threshold is 0.71 — meaning the model only fires when it's extremely confident. In practice, EURUSD signals will be rare (maybe 1–2 per month at that threshold). XAUUSD fires more often (threshold 0.50). That is by design: **quality over quantity**. The goal is that when the system says "trade this," it's worth trading. No signal = no trade = protect the account.
